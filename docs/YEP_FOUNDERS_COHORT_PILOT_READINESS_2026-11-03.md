# A/1 Suppliers — YEP Founders Cohort Pilot: Operations and App Acceptance

Working field plan | 2026-11-03 | DRAFT: no participant enrollment or launch is implied

## Lock the model

- Organization: A/1 Suppliers, Young Entrepreneurs Process (YEP). "Process," not "Program."
- Initial pilot planning size: **15 youth**, **8 weeks**, **2 facilitated sessions per week** = **16 sessions**. These are targets, not confirmed attendance or a scheduled launch.
- YEP ages 7–17: Foundation (7–10), Builder (11–13), Momentum (14–17). Young Adult Entrepreneur Process (Y.A.E.P.) ages 18–24 is a distinct lane; do not combine minors and adults into one unsupervised learning group.
- The app is a visual learning engine, **not a replacement for facilitated discussion, workbook reflection, parental consent or safeguarding**.
- Teaching sequence: **SEE → UNDERSTAND → INTERACT → RESPOND → REFLECT → FINISH**. Four lanes are Daily Quest, S.T.E.M.S (legacy route key remains stemSin), Mirror Results and FINISHER Mission.
- First field test should be with **fictional personas only**, using a stable preview hostname, before any youth encounter.

## Pilot app release boundary

This is a **demo PWA**. Saved information is held in browser-local storage at a particular URL on a particular device. It is not a secure institutional youth roster, cross-device tracker, parent consent system, or staff authentication system. Do not enter real child names, contact information, sensitive reflections or real youth attendance data in this preview. Keep real cohort intake in an approved, access-controlled system with appropriate retention and role limits. App self-marked steps are not independently verified outcomes.

### First test, before youth enrollment

1. Open the same authorized preview build on Tablet 01 and Tablet 02. Use disposable fictional test profiles and record device/browser/build.
2. Verify **Foundation**: Daily Quest → S.T.E.M.S → Mirror Results → FINISHER Mission → reflection. Test illustrated choices, tapping scene stages, guide speech, question playback, stop, replay, and screen reader text.
3. Stop mid-lesson, refresh, close/reopen browser and verify saved progress. Try warm-cache offline reopen; distinguish offline visual content from device-dependent/offline voice availability.
4. Repeat a short mission on Builder, Momentum, and Y.A.E.P. Verify language and character maturity, appropriate age decisions and isolation between modes.
5. Verify no youth microphone permission requests, overlapping spoken audio, false completion, or missing state after returning home.
6. Record actual failures with steps to reproduce, the exact preview URL, device, screenshots without personal information, and a correction/owner.
7. Sign off only when both devices pass and a real facilitator can direct a session without needing developer assistance.

## Sixteen-session structure (adapt for learner age)

| Week | Session 1 | Session 2 | Expected learning evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Welcome, Power Name, identity and safe group norms | Spot needs and interests | Baseline observation and problem statement |
| 2 | Who do businesses help? People and customers | Daily Quest: recognize a real problem | Selected problem and who it affects |
| 3 | Business exposure: operator/mentor or approved case | Choices, constraints and an idea sketch | Question asked, stakeholder insight, first idea |
| 4 | S.T.E.M.S: tools and problem-solving | Prototype/predict/test safely | Before/after comparison, honest observations |
| 5 | Value: product, service and resource choices | Budget and trade-offs, age appropriate | Explanation of cost/time/value trade-off |
| 6 | Communicate an idea and get permitted feedback | Improve one version of the idea | Difference between original and revised idea |
| 7 | Mirror Results: what happened and why | FINISHER: choose, plan and take a supervised step | Self-reported reflection plus facilitator observation |
| 8 | Prepare an authentic learning demonstration | Showcase, reflection, feedback and next step | Baseline-to-end comparison and optional approved showcase |

For younger children, use illustrations and facilitator-guided demonstrations. Older teens get case-based decisions. Y.A.E.P. receives separately designed adult applications. Visiting businesses, taking photos, public interviews, selling food and real customer contact are not assumed pilot activities; each requires the right permissions and approvals.

## Measurement: the evidence needed to call the pilot successful

Predefine thresholds with the facilitator and board before launch. At minimum report:
- Number enrolled, consented, attended (per session), retained through Week 8, and reason for dropout when offered voluntarily.
- Number who independently navigate the four learning lanes with age-appropriate assistance, and number who needed intervention.
- Baseline and follow-up ability to identify a need, person affected, useful idea, next step, and what evidence actually shows.
- Real participant/facilitator feedback and documented app failures. Separate a simulated completed mission from a completed real-world action.
- Actual spending and total cost per participant, clearly marked as measured, not budget assumption.

Do **not** call a learner a graduate, certify employment readiness, claim revenue creation, or use personal case stories as impact evidence without corroboration and permissions.

## Operating sequence

1. **Now:** App regression, preview deployment and physical Tablet 01/02 pass.
2. **In parallel:** Richmond verifies site and schedule; Unk verifies facilitation; operations lead finalizes consent, staffing, safety, accessibility and intake.
3. **Before cohort kickoff:** One facilitator-led dry run and enrollment reconciliation. Stop if a child is entered into demo storage or audio unexpectedly activates the microphone.
4. **During pilot:** Weekly read-only dashboard for verified host/session status, attendance totals, educational evidence, unresolved issues and named next action.
5. **After Week 8:** Funders/partners receive aggregate, permission-safe outcomes rather than raw youth records or invented success claims.

## CEO go/no-go

**HOLD** any real-youth app onboarding until child privacy, authorized cohort intake, audio on both physical tablets, and facilitator procedures are proven. A visual technical POC and a Vercel success badge alone do not authorize a live youth pilot.
